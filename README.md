# STK1110 – Statistical Methods and Data Analysis

This repository contains my work for **STK1110 – Statistical Methods and Data Analysis**, a course at the University of Oslo.

**Course Information:** [STK1110 – Statistical Methods and Data Analysis](https://www.uio.no/studier/emner/matnat/math/STK1110/index-eng.html)

## Course Description

STK1110 provides an introduction to statistical thinking and an overview of central statistical models and methods. The course gives insight into the theoretical foundation of methods for analyzing data, and students learn to perform such analyses in practice using the software **R**. STK1110 is a natural continuation of STK1100 and provides a foundation for taking advanced courses in statistics.

### Learning Outcomes

After completing this course, students will be able to:

- Understand the reasoning behind point and interval estimation, find moment estimators and maximum likelihood estimators for simple statistical models, and know the properties of maximum likelihood estimators for large sample situations
- Understand the reasoning behind hypothesis testing, choose and perform tests, perform power calculations for simple hypothesis testing problems, and know the likelihood ratio test and its properties in large sample situations
- Analyze pairwise data and data for two samples, and understand the theoretical foundation for the statistical methods
- Use linear and logistic regression for studying the connection between a response variable and one or more explanatory variables, understand the theoretical foundation for simple linear regression, and know how the theory of multiple linear regression can be studied using matrix algebra
- Understand the reasoning behind parametric and non-parametric bootstrap, and use bootstrap to calculate standard error and interval estimates
- Know the ideas behind non-parametric inference and Bayesian analysis

## Course Details

- **Credits:** 10
- **Level:** Bachelor
- **Teaching:** Autumn semester
- **Examination:** Autumn semester
- **Teaching Language:** Norwegian
- **Teaching Format:** 4 hours of lectures and 2 hours of exercise sessions in groups per week

### Prerequisites

**Recommended previous knowledge:**
- STK1100 – Probability and Statistical Modelling
- MAT1100 – Calculus
- MAT1110 – Calculus and Linear Algebra
- MAT-INF1100 / MAT1105 – Linear algebra and numerical methods
- IN1900 – Introduction to Programming with Scientific Applications

### Examination

- Final written exam counts 100% towards the final grade
- 2 mandatory assignments must be approved before sitting the final exam
- **Examination support material:** Formula sheet for STK1100/STK1110 and approved calculators are allowed
- **Language of examination:** Norwegian (responses may be submitted in Norwegian, Swedish, Danish, or English)
- **Grading scale:** A to F (A is the best grade, F is a fail)
